Madalas na tanong (FAQ)

Kailangan ba STEM ang SHS strand?

Hindi required. May bridging program kami ng 1 sem para sa non-STEM (HUMSS, ABM, TVL). Basta may basic math at willing matuto, welcome ka.

May online / hybrid ba?

Meron. Lahat ng programs may hybrid option: 2 days on-campus for labs, the rest online live sessions + recorded. Perfect sa working students.

Anong laptop ang kailangan?

For 1st year, kahit i5 / 8GB RAM okay na. May laptop loan program kami (₱1,500/mo). Pag AI/Robotics, may lab machines na high-end — hindi mo kailangan bumili agad ng mamahalin.

Guaranteed ba ang trabaho?

94% employed within 6 months (2024 tracer study). May Job Guarantee Program: kung hindi ka ma-hire in 6 months after grad (with conditions), libre ang extra upskilling hanggang ma-hire ka.

Paano ang scholarship?

Upon application, auto-assess ka na for ECAST Merit. For DOST/CHED, tutulungan ka ng scholarship office mag-apply. Deadline for A.Y. 2026-2027: April 15, 2026.

Handa ka na bang buuin ang kinabukasan?

2,400+ ECASTians na ang nag-aaral. Ikaw na lang ang kulang. Application is free — 5 minutes lang.
